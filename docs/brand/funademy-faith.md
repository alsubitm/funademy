# FunAdemy Faith — what we believe and how we show it

Owner-approved, 27 Nov 2026. Every creative helper (copy, design, campaign) follows this.

## The heart
**فكرة صغيرة وخيال كبير** — *Small idea, big imagination.*
Every child starts with a small spark — a question, a "what if?", a few bricks — and FunAdemy helps it grow into big imagination and real achievement.

Brand line: **هنا ينطلق الفضول** — *Let curiosity fly.*

## Our values (show at least one in every piece)
1. **Curiosity & imagination** — «وش يصير لو؟». Questions and wild ideas are welcome.
2. **Hands-on, real skills** — children really build, engineer, program and test. They make; they don't just watch.
3. **Joy & confidence** — the «أنا سويتها!» moment. Mistakes are part of the fun: try, adjust, try again.
4. **Saudi pride & future** — «أطفالنا يبنون وطننا»: we are raising the young engineers who will build the nation's future.

## The FunAdemy Way (our story in three steps)
1. **تخيّلها!** — Imagine it: start with a question, a challenge or an idea nobody tried.
2. **يلا نسويها!** — Make it happen: build, code and test with friends.
3. **أنا سويتها!** — I did it: test, rethink, try again — every win makes you want the next one.

«نتعلّم من جدّ. وننبسط من جدّ.» — The learning is real. So is the joy.

## Personality: playful & warm
- Feels like a happy discovery club, not a strict school.
- Bright, friendly, encouraging, never pushy or scary; never shame a child or a parent.
- Arabic first, in natural warm Saudi dialect (e.g. «يلا»، «وش»، «يا سلام»), short sentences; English versions are friendly and simple.

## Visual identity ("Bright Play")
- Colours: purple `#7C3AED` (buttons, main accents), deep purple `#3B1878`, yellow `#FFCA17` (highlights, sparks), navy `#101A38` (text, outlines), warm paper-white `#F8F6EF` (backgrounds), sand `#FBF5E8` (dividers).
- Logo: the gold **Kite-Cap** diamond — a flying kite (play) that becomes a graduation cap (learning). Play and learning in one mark.
- Mascot: **Nujaym (نُجَيْم)** — a friendly teal wheeled robot holding a golden star. Curious, cheerful, helpful.
- Imagery: real-looking Saudi children building, coding and smiling. Children wear casual everyday clothes; traditional dress (hijab, shemagh, thobe, abaya) only on adults. Bright, airy, hands and bricks in action.
- Big readable Arabic text (right-to-left), short headlines (6 words or fewer on images).

## Never
- Never invent reviews, testimonials, student numbers, results, awards, partnerships or government/NELC accreditation.
- Never imply LEGO endorses FunAdemy (LEGO® may describe the bricks). e² Young Engineers is credited as the programme licensor.
- Never present AI-generated people as real FunAdemy students or staff.
- No screen-addiction imagery, no fear-based messages, no tiny text.
