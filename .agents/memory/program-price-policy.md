---
name: Program tuition policy
description: Why public program fees are quoted per enrollment run rather than by teaching format.
---

Quote base monthly tuition per available enrollment run, not one fee per language or delivery format. Keep discounts separate from the base quote.

**Why:** historical Arabic and English format-based labels disagreed with enrollment fees. Listing individually identified runs avoids implying that a cheaper run's price applies to every schedule.

**How to apply:** future pricing redesigns should preserve run identity and distinguish no available runs from a failed price lookup; checkout remains the authority at purchase time.
