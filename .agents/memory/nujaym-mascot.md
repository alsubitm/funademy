Nujaym (نُجَيْم) is FunAdemy's conversational engineering mascot. Use the owner's
supplied teal wheeled robot holding a golden star, not an invented replacement.
The launcher is “اسأل نُجَيْم” / “Ask Nujaym”, lower-left in both reading directions.

**Why:** The owner supplied the mascot image and implementation brief explicitly
requiring reuse of the existing chat and that artwork.

**How to apply:** Reply in friendly clear Saudi Arabic for Arabic questions and
English for English questions, independently of page language. Use published
catalogue ages for recommendations; never invent fees, schedules or seats.
Collect booking/contact details through the existing forms, not the chat.
The signature activity asks about two directly meshing gears and celebrates a
correct opposite-direction answer with one golden-star glow. Respect reduced
motion, chat consent and private-account boundaries.
