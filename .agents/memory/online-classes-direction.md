Adapt the existing FunAdemy project, not a separate PHP classroom or a new
Learning Hub project. Follow the three phases in the Online Classes brief:
finish and test Phase 1 before expanding to Phase 2, then Phase 3.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected “Adapt this existing FunAdemy project”
after being told the brief named a different project and technology stack.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing Next.js, Prisma, PostgreSQL, family accounts,
payments, teaching tools and Windmill. Do not replace the stack solely to
match the brief's assumed NestJS/Redis architecture.

New courses are live, tutor-led online classes starting January 2027.
“Bright Play” supersedes the exact V3 appearance: warm paper-white, purple
primary buttons, yellow highlights, navy text/outlines only, sand dividers.
Children wear casual clothing; hijab and shemagh appear only on adults.
Keep the kite-to-cap identity animation, with reduced-motion support.

**Why:** These are explicit requirements in the newly approved brief and
supersede earlier in-person-only, blue-button and traditional-child-clothing
instructions.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing accounts, bookings and historical venues.
FunAdemy is the sole header/title/email/certificate brand; licensor identity,
programmes and lesson content belong in configuration, with the credit once
in the footer. Prices and offers must be owner-editable data, not UI literals.
Do not declare the platform compliant merely because the homepage changed.

Saudi-hosted personal data and a limited registration field list are explicit
release requirements. The owner approved optional parent email after the
conflict between email confirmations and the restricted field list was raised.
Send email confirmations only when an address is supplied. District selection
still conflicts with the ban on place names other than Riyadh.

**Why:** The owner selected “Allow optional parent email.” Other silent
assumptions would change consent, data collection or the residency requirement.

**How to apply:** Do not delete existing identity records, relocate the
database, or claim Saudi residency/Fatoora compliance without verification
and the required owner decisions.
