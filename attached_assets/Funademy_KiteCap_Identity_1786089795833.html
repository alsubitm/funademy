01 · THE IDEA — TRY IT YOURSELF

One diamond, two truths

A mortarboard seen from above is a diamond. A diamond with a tail is a kite. So the Funademy mark is both at once: fun flying on the outside, academy hiding in plain sight. Hover (or tap) the mark below — the kite lands and becomes the cap.

Why this is outside the box:

It's not a symbol about the brand — it's the brand promise working as an optical illusion. Fun (the kite) and Academy (the cap) are literally the same shape, which is exactly Funademy's claim: play and learning are one thing. It animates natively (kites fly, sway, land), it's unlike anything in Saudi education, and the two-mode system gives you a playful mark for marketing and a dignified one for certificates — same geometry, zero confusion.
