FAQ · أسئلة الأهالي

Questions parents ask

What happens in the free trial class?
Your child joins one complete live session with the group — building or coding alongside the other kids. You watch, they play, and afterwards we recommend the right program and level. No payment details needed.
Is the online class really live, or recorded videos?
100% live, every time. A certified instructor teaches 8–12 children in real time, sees their screens, calls them by name, and adapts to each child. There are no recorded lessons.
Which program is right for my child's age?
Follow the Learning Path above: ages 4–6 start with Big Builders or Smartivo, 6–10 with Bricks Challenge, 7–11 with AlgoPlay/RoboToys, 8–12 with Galileo Technic, and 10–15 with AlgoC. The free trial confirms the perfect fit.
What does my child need for online classes?
A computer or tablet, a stable internet connection, and a quiet spot. We send a simple setup guide on WhatsApp before the first session — most families are ready in five minutes.
Do you serve my neighborhood?
We're the official Young Engineers operator for Riyadh North: Al Yasmin, An Narjis, Al Sahafah, An Nada, An Nafal, Al Falah, Al Ghadir, At Taawun, and King Salman Neighborhood. Outside these? Message us and we'll direct you to your local operator.
Is this the official Young Engineers program?
Yes — Falcon Eye Ltd is the official licensee of e² Young Engineers for the Riyadh North territory. Your child follows the same global curriculum and progression, with certificates on the official pathway.
